## 13. How to Apply / Verify

### Step-by-Step: Getting Started

1. **Get your UofC email active** — Make sure your `@ucalgary.ca` email is set up and accessible.

2. **Apply for GitHub Student Developer Pack first** — This unlocks the most offers:
   - Go to https://education.github.com/pack
   - Sign in with your GitHub account (`Amiirhosseini`)
   - Upload your UofC student ID or enrollment verification
   - Wait for approval (usually 1–7 days)

3. **Verify on individual platforms** — For services not in the GitHub pack:
   - Use your `.ucalgary.ca` email to sign up
   - Some may require uploading a student ID or enrollment letter
   - Enrollment letters can be obtained from your MyUofC portal

4. **Check UofC IT portal** — Visit https://ucalgary.ca/information-technologies for UofC-provided software.

5. **Renew annually** — Most student offers expire after 1 year. Set a calendar reminder to renew before expiry.

### Documents You May Need
- UofC Student ID card (photo)
- Enrollment verification letter (from MyUofC > Student Centre)
- `.ucalgary.ca` email address
- UCID number
